## What a result must mean

Keep these outcomes separate in code, reports and the pitch:

| Outcome | Permitted meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Automated measurement | The program calculated a value from identified input or generated geometry, with the applicable scope stated. |
| Reviewer observation | A person supplied a measurement, identity, completeness statement or approval with source references. It is not automatically extracted CAD evidence. |
| Pass | This particular check passed on the identified route and supplied evidence. It does not imply complete drawing coverage or full-system approval. |
| Fail | The identified geometry or observation violates the particular mandatory rule. |
| Preference finding | Outside a recommendation that the brief permits exceeding; it must not silently become either a mandatory rejection or a full pass. |
| Needs review | Evidence, interpretation or an implementation is missing. This is not a completed automated check. |
| Not assessed / unsupported | The component or section was not checked. It stays in the coverage denominator and must be locatable. |
| No better route found | A bounded search found no better accepted alternative. This does not prove global optimality. |
| Ready for geometric review | A mesh and its implemented geometry checks are ready. This does not mean that matching commercial parts, installation sequence or the entire system have been approved. |

The baseline already preserves many of these distinctions. However, `challenge_rules.assess_layout` can issue passes for some **reviewer declarations**, and its always-pending requirements mean that merely listing all rules is not equivalent to checking all of them. `model_context.coverage_declared_complete` is explicitly a declaration. Every report should retain the evidence origin, route/source binding and coverage beside the result.

## Interpretation decisions that software must not invent

1. **Pipe size categories:** the brief says 50, 80 and 100 mm size groups, not measured OD. The nominal-size interpretation needs source evidence. Sizes between the listed groups have no stated rule; no interpolation is authorized by the text.
2. **Pitch:** the brief specifies 1:25 and 1:100, not merely a universal minimum 1:100. Exact tolerance and diagonal/run-through-bend handling are not supplied. The baseline separates minimum-fall screening from exact-pitch review.
3. **Penetration clearance:** 2 in is 50.8 mm, while the paragraph says 50 mm. Use a disclosed conservative 50.8 mm policy or obtain the organizer's preferred metric; do not silently mix it with shock clearance or claim the inconsistency is resolved.
4. **Headroom:** 77 in is 1955.8 mm, while the brief publishes 1960 mm. The baseline uses the more conservative explicit 1960 mm value.
5. **Deck/bulkhead offset:** the source prohibits a straight run between two such crossings and calls for a one-diameter direction change. It does not define an offset measurement algorithm or whether diameter means nominal or outside diameter. It is not a generic one-diameter clearance from a deck or wall.
6. **Lay-flat:** no planarity tolerance is supplied. The generated-geometry 0.01 mm value is a prototype assumption, not an organizer requirement.
7. **Supports and shock clearance:** a horizontal hanger measurement starts at pipe edge while a vertical one starts at its centre. Actual support attachment/contact is intentional and needs identified exceptions; automatic obstacle waivers are not an interpretation of the brief.
8. **Piece and weld boundaries:** displayed mesh components are not necessarily manufactured spools, and mesh interfaces are not automatically welds. An approved decomposition must support length, bend-count, spacing and installation checks together.
9. **Function and catalog suitability:** geometric pipe diameter/radius alone does not identify material, pressure rating, mating ends or a suitable commercial fitting. Missing service/catalog information remains a concrete designer-intervention item.
10. **Whole system:** the drawing may contain cylindrical valve internals, fasteners and fittings. A geometric cylinder inventory is not automatically an inventory of reroutable pipe lengths. Unsupported and ambiguous objects must be accounted for without moving them as pipes.

## One-by-one implementation order and stopping gates

These gates prioritize actual requirement coverage. A gate is complete only after the listed test and the affected matrix rows are updated. A temporary reviewer-intervention result is a useful honest outcome, but does not mark the associated mandatory implementation as finished.

1. **Complete inventory and resumable screening** — SYS-03, ROUTE-02/03, OUT-05. Remove silent first-page coverage limitations. Every source section/object gets a result; preserve stable IDs, progress and resumability. Demonstrate on a fixture over the old 50-section limit and the supplied drawing.
2. **Identified engineering context and measured checks** — SYS-06, ROUTE-09/11/13, ACCESS-01–08, FAB-13/14. Add source-bound geometry records for spaces, walkways/ladders, structure/penetrations, valve stems and supports. Compute measurements where geometry exists. Do not satisfy the gate by adding `verified: true` flags.
3. **Pitched-system output** — SYS-07, ROUTE-05–08, FAB-18. Produce a continuous pitched proposal with verified flow/axes and final-shape validation. Prove pressure and drain cases through the same AutoCAD workflow.
