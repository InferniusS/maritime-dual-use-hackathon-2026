# PipeForge Edge 5.0 engineering record

Version 5.0 extends the existing sanitized `pipeforge-edge-v4` implementation at `22ad50e425c441befae323360de0034983c45869`. It preserves the original history boundary, existing services, original six connected examples, CAD inspection, engineering standards profile and reviewed exports. No `main` history or drawing-derived assets are imported. The original 4.0 audit and installation record remain in [V4_0.md](V4_0.md).

## Coordinated optimization

The existing finite-grid router generates geometry; a separate whole-network validator decides acceptance. Network nodes retain equipment ports, valves, tees/junctions, explicit incidences, systems, dimensions, source handles and drawing revision. Fixed equipment stays fixed. Movable components use only designer-permitted regions and sampled declared positions. Proximity still creates a question requiring confirmation rather than an implicit connection.

The 5.0 search combines these existing capabilities with several bounded routing orders and two planning modes. Retained-space passes keep surrounding source routes active. Prioritized-reservation passes temporarily release only selected, modifiable routes that have not yet been planned. Each newly routed finite pipe and fitting envelope then becomes a shared reservation for subsequent spans. Locked and unselected systems remain obstacles throughout. Permitted connected-node variants reroute every affected incident span; incumbent rerouting can refine an accepted network.

Temporary release is a search operation, never acceptance permission. Every complete candidate is independently checked against every retained span, other piping system, component and supplied obstacle. A new clash anywhere rejects the complete proposal. Routes do not become valid by being individually shorter. The original snapshot remains immutable; cancellation discards the proposal.

The result is **heuristically improved** or **feasible but not proven optimal**. It is not a global multi-commodity optimum. `NO FEASIBLE ROUTE FOUND` means no acceptable candidate was established within the stated discrete, evaluation and time budgets. It does not prove continuous-space infeasibility. Scenario 08 supplies a literal sealed cross-section, but the general search status still carries this limitation.

## Objectives and evidence

Six strategies are available: `balanced`, `length`, `bends`, `hydraulic`, `minimal_modifications` and `feasibility`. Custom nonnegative weights can name length, bends, elevation, hydraulic loss, modifications and constructability. Every numerical term is normalized by stable scales from the immutable source; the total score is dimensionless. Measured geometry, raw values, normalization scales, effective weights and individual contributions accompany the result. Hard checks gate acceptance separately from the score.

Hydraulic evidence uses Darcy–Weisbach straight-pipe friction plus supplied bend-loss coefficients at explicitly supplied fixed flows. Inner diameter, fluid density, viscosity, flow, roughness, coefficient and source evidence are required. Missing evidence remains UNVERIFIED and its objective contribution is omitted explicitly; a fully unsupported objective reports a fallback. The estimate does not solve branch flows, network pressures, pump duty, valve losses or hydraulic compliance. Modification and constructability terms are declared geometric proxies rather than verified cost or installation labor.

The deterministic engineering explanation names changed spans and systems, actual measured constraints, coordinated changes, candidate tradeoffs and unresolved evidence. No external AI service is required or used as a geometry or compliance authority.

## Independent engineering validation

Original and proposed geometry use the same criteria. Each check reports PASS, FAIL, UNVERIFIED or NOT_APPLICABLE with measured and required values when available. Source-preservation checks retain fixed ports, topology, systems, diameters, declared component dimensions, fitting metadata and CAD identity. Physical checks cover finite pipe diameter, sampled physical elbow sweeps, components, conservative CAD/unknown obstacles, self-interference and cross-system interference. Port-contact exemptions are explicitly local and bounded; they do not erase distant connected geometry.

The versioned [standards profile](../../standards/fincantieri-track2-user-transcription-v1.json) is a user transcription of the challenge requirements. An original official document and approved manufacturer catalog have not been independently verified in this repository. No additional requirement is presented as an official Fincantieri standard.

| Requirement represented | Implemented interpretation and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Shock clearance | 50.8 mm finite-envelope separation from represented structure, equipment and distributed systems. Incomplete geometry coverage prevents a complete environment claim. |
| Access | 1,960 mm headroom over supplied walkway footprints using explicit ship-up coordinates. Ladder/access volumes are protected obstacles; collision checks alone do not prove installation access. |
| Valves | Declared stem orientation and height are checked against the transcribed limits; permitted moves preserve body dimensions and recompute geometry-dependent evidence. Missing orientation, elevation or access data stays unresolved. |
| Bend table | DN ≤50: radius 152.4 mm and net straight 101.6 mm; DN80: 228.6/177.8 mm; DN ≥100: 304.8/228.6 mm. Intermediate nominal sizes are not interpolated. Actual catalog radius and tangent cutback determine physical checks. |
| Manufacturing | Proposed piece lengths ≤6,096 mm, weld spacing ≥25 mm and at most three bends per proposed piece; bend increments, takeouts, generated joints and piece continuity are checked. A geometric piece schedule is not a fabrication release. |
| Drainage | Flow-directed segment checks use actual orthonormal ship axes: fore-and-aft 1:25, athwartship 1:100. Local reverse slope cannot be hidden by sufficient total endpoint drop. |
| Supports/penetrations | Supplied evidence is evaluated, but missing hanger/support/structure information stays UNVERIFIED. The source's penetration-unit discrepancy remains unresolved. |
| Fittings | Physical radius, envelope, allowed angle and connection-size evidence are checked. Missing or explicitly unavailable fitting evidence cannot be silently replaced by demo dimensions. Provisional synthetic catalogs are not commercial availability or ship approval. |

Missing material, wall thickness, pressure rating, approved catalogs, supports, tolerances, installation sequence, complete CAD interpretation or engineering authority remain explicit. An accepted geometric candidate can still require extensive engineering review.

## Product and CAD workflow

The connected workspace supports multiple span/system selection, all-reviewed-network selection, six strategies, candidate/time limits, background progress and cooperative cancellation. The 3D workspace offers system colors/isolation, changed/unchanged controls, original/proposed overlay and side-by-side comparison, object inspection, exact WCS coordinates, dimensions, collision markers, camera presets, context transparency and a point-to-point measurement tool. Topology and calculation-backed recommendations remain available with missing evidence visible.

The additive native AutoCAD command is `PIPEFORGEOPTIMIZE`. It uses a reviewed CAD network with source handles/instances, explicit topology and revision identity, supports multiple traced CAD objects or systems, and offers calculation, cancellation, review, approval and local export. Source solids are preserved. Optional persistent review centerlines are a separate undoable transaction after explicit approval; they are not replacement solids or inserted commercial fittings. See [AUTOCAD_5.md](AUTOCAD_5.md) for exact Windows commands and native acceptance checks.

The plugin project cross-compiled with .NET SDK 10.0.100 and AutoCAD.NET 26.0.0 references with zero warnings/errors. Native AutoCAD execution is **unverified** in this Linux environment. HTTP protocol tests and a successful build do not establish native preview, dialogs, save/Undo or installed Windows behavior.

Native extraction selects model-space, non-Xref `3DSOLID`/`INSERT` sources and retains surrounding context. Existing DXF inspection and optional STEP paths remain as documented in their original import workflows. An unsupported or uncertain solid is preserved as a conservative obstacle or an explicit coverage uncertainty; arbitrary CAD solids are not promised semantic pipe recognition. Confirm actual units, coordinates, dimensions, systems, connections and revision before optimization. The legacy 2025/2026 connector retains its separate compatibility scope.

Reviewed deliverables comprise eight local files: `network-result.json`, `instructions.json`, `instructions.md`, `components.xlsx`, `components.csv`, `coordinates.csv`, `network-preview.dxf` and `network-comparison.svg`. They retain source handles/instances, systems, original/proposed coordinates, diameters, component/fitting requirements, connection identities, validation and engineering notes. Workbooks include coordinates, manufacturing/weld schedules, per-span/system change audits, candidate comparisons, baseline/final checks and installation-review steps. A serialized round-trip audit checks coordinates across JSON, XLSX, CSV and DXF. Static SVG is a dimensioned comparison, not a fabrication drawing. Unknown parts and approval details remain unresolved.

Scenario 09 exercises actual coordinated release/reservation. Its cooling line has a declared 600 mm bend radius; the firemain has 200 mm. Keeping firemain in place prevents the short cooling underpass from satisfying the large radius. Optimizing cooling alone finds no improvement in this search. Joint optimization takes cooling directly through the corridor and moves firemain to a valid finite-envelope underpass. Both source systems were already valid, both change, and the entire proposed network passes implemented hard checks. No component is resized to obtain this result.

Scenario 10 is an entirely invented 8 × 6 × 4 m machinery space with three systems, equipment, duct, wireway, walkway and ladder-access volumes. It is representative demonstration geometry, not a drawing or a claim about a particular vessel.

Every accepted proposal in the final ten-scenario run had **zero independently measured hard failures**. Scenarios 07 and 08 produce no proposal and retain their invalid original geometry and measured failures; retaining a source is not a PASS for that source. Unselected spans and systems remain part of the full validation, even though selection prevents the optimizer from modifying them. A result's release status and UNVERIFIED findings remain separate from its search status.

## Reproduce and measure

From an installed environment at the repository root:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_v5_scenarios.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/benchmark_v5.py --repeats 3 --max-evaluations 16 --output local-output/v5-benchmark.json
.venv/bin/python scripts/benchmark_v5.py --scenario 09-coordinated-improvement --repeats 3 --measure-memory --output local-output/v5-coordinated.json
```

On Windows substitute `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe` and backslash paths. The benchmark checks source immutability and performs another independent validation outside the timed search. Cold runs clear the route-geometry cache; warm runs retain it. Neither clears Python import or operating-system caches. Allocation tracing is optional, in a separate warm pass, and measures Python allocation peak rather than process RSS. Reports remain local and are not fixture outputs or committed artifacts.

The final sequential three-repeat run on the Linux CPython 3.12.14/x86-64 development host, with no concurrent test suite, measured:
